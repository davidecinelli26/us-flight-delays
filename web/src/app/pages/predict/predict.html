AUC is the chance that a delayed flight gets a higher score than a punctual one (0.5 = guessing). Brier score and log loss measure the probabilities themselves: lower is better. Most of what makes a given flight late, the weather that day and the state of the network, is not known when the ticket is bought, so no model of this kind gets close to certainty: the point is to rank flights by risk and give honest probabilities.
