About this dashboard

How the numbers were made, and by whom.

Data

  • Reporting Carrier On-Time Performance, Bureau of Transportation Statistics, US Department of Transportation: one row per scheduled domestic flight of the airlines that report to the DOT, {{ flights() }}. The 2013 files are the ones of the original course project; 2024 shows how things changed.
  • Airport names and coordinates from OurAirports (public domain).
  • Map borders from Natural Earth (public domain), shipped with the site: the map loads nothing from third-party servers.
  • Charts by Chart.js, map by Leaflet.

Definitions

  • On time: arrived less than 15 minutes after the scheduled time. Delayed: 15 minutes or more.
  • Every scheduled flight is exactly one of on time, delayed, cancelled or diverted, so the four shares add up to 100%.
  • Mean delay is over the flights that landed at their destination, with early arrivals counted as negative.
  • Delay causes are reported by the airlines for flights 15 minutes late or more, since June 2003.
  • Distances are in statute miles, as published by BTS.

How it is built

BTS monthly zips ──► Spark (Scala) ──► small JSON tables ──► this Angular app
 checksum-verified    clean · aggregate     web/public/data         static, on GitHub Pages
                      train the model

An Apache Spark job reads every flight, computes the aggregates shown here and trains the delay model with Spark MLlib. The dashboard only reads the results, so it needs no server. Source code, tests and the full method are on GitHub.
